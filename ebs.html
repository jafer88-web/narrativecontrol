Phase 1: Foundation Video

90 SECONDS

"This Is Our Story" - Direct-to-camera reaffirmation with emotional montage

1 Scene 1: Direct Address (0-10s)

Visual

Trusted host on camera. Orange gradient backdrop. Casual professional.

Script

"Lately, we've seen many voices speaking about EBS. Today, we want to speak directly to you—our audience..."

Execution

Apparel: Neutral colors. Audio: Clear, calm. Text: None (pure direct address).

2 Scene 2: Proof Montage (10-65s)

A. Joy Reel (15s)

Fast cuts of entertainment. Orange text: "15 Years of Shared Laughter".

B. Impact Reel (20s)

Humanitarian footage. Text: "A Voice Where It's Needed Most".

C. People Reel (15s)

User-generated content. Text: "Your Stories. Your Platform."

Key: Rapid cuts (0.5-1s each). Text appears for 2s. Emotional crescendo.

3 Scene 3: Humble Bridge (65-75s)

Visual & Tone

Return to host, closer shot. Soft white background. Intimate, sincere.

Script

"We are not a perfect channel. We are a passionate one. Every day, our only goal is to serve you..."

4 Scene 4: Call to Action (75-90s)

Visual

Final supercut. Ends on powerful wide shot. Bold orange and white text.

Script & Text

"This is our story. But it doesn't end here."

Community Management Protocol

Do NOT

  • Engage in arguments or quote-tweet negativity
  • Use defensive or aggressive language
  • Acknowledge specific false narratives directly

DO

  • Like, share, comment on positive UGC
  • Use standard polite reply for negative comments: "We're sorry to hear you feel that way. Our goal has always been to serve our audience with integrity. We'd love to hear what you enjoy watching on your next visit. 💛"
